# FACEBOOM Community Guidelines

FACEBOOM is a place to connect, share and communicate. Everyone who uses FACEBOOM is expected to help keep the community safe, respectful and lawful.

These guidelines apply to profiles, posts, photos, videos, comments, messages and other activity on FACEBOOM. They are separate from Facebook's rules.

## 1. Treat people with respect
- Do not threaten, bully, harass, stalk or intimidate anyone.
- Do not encourage hatred, dehumanization or violence against a person or group.
- Do not repeatedly target someone with unwanted contact or abusive content.

## 2. Protect people from harm
- Do not share child sexual abuse material, exploit or endanger children, or sexualize minors.
- Do not share intimate images or sexual content of another person without their consent.
- Do not make credible threats or encourage violence or self-harm.
- Do not glorify, promote or facilitate exploitation, abuse or human trafficking.

## 3. Be honest and protect privacy
- Do not impersonate another person, organization or business in a deceptive way.
- Do not scam, phish, defraud, distribute malware or trick people into revealing passwords or financial details.
- Do not publish private information, account credentials, personal contact details or identifying information without permission.
- Do not use spam, fake engagement, automated abuse or coordinated manipulation.

## 4. Share lawful content
- Do not use FACEBOOM to offer illegal goods or services, or to promote illegal activity.
- Only upload content you created or have permission and legal rights to share.
- Do not evade restrictions by creating replacement accounts or moving prohibited activity to another feature.

## 5. Reporting and blocking
Use the in-app **Report** option on relevant posts, comments or profiles where available. Use **Block / Unblock** to limit unwanted interactions. For concerns that cannot be reported in the app, contact faceboom.support@gmail.com. Never email passwords or verification codes.

## 6. Review of reports
Authorized FACEBOOM moderators may review reported content, account information and available context. A report does not guarantee enforcement. FACEBOOM aims to review reports fairly and consistently, while protecting privacy.

## 7. Enforcement
Depending on severity, context and history, FACEBOOM may remove content, issue a warning, restrict features, temporarily suspend an account or permanently disable an account. Severe or urgent violations may result in action without a prior warning. FACEBOOM makes its own enforcement decisions; Facebook does not automatically decide FACEBOOM account status.

## 8. Appeals
To appeal a content or account decision, email faceboom.support@gmail.com with the subject **FACEBOOM Moderation Appeal**. Include your FACEBOOM username, the date of the action and a short explanation. Never send your password. An authorized person will review the appeal where possible; the decision may be upheld, changed or reversed.

## 9. Urgent danger
FACEBOOM email is not an emergency service or real-time emergency channel. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority in your location. Do not wait for an email response.

## 10. Contact
Community Guidelines questions, safety reports and moderation appeals: **faceboom.support@gmail.com**.

FACEBOOM may update these guidelines as the service and applicable requirements change.
