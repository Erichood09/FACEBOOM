FACEBOOM Community Guidelines

FACEBOOM is a place to connect, share and communicate. Everyone who uses FACEBOOM is expected to help keep the community safe, respectful and lawful.

These guidelines apply to profiles, posts, photos, videos, comments, messages and other activity on FACEBOOM. They are separate from Facebook's rules.

1. Treat people with respect

  • Do not threaten, bully, harass, stalk or intimidate anyone.
  • Do not encourage hatred, dehumanization or violence against a person or group.
  • Do not repeatedly target someone with unwanted contact or abusive content.

2. Protect people from harm

  • Do not share child sexual abuse material, exploit or endanger children, or sexualize minors.
  • Do not share intimate images or sexual content of another person without their consent.
  • Do not make credible threats or encourage violence or self-harm.
  • Do not glorify, promote or facilitate exploitation, abuse or human trafficking.

3. Be honest and protect privacy

  • Do not impersonate another person, organization or business in a deceptive way.
  • Do not scam, phish, defraud, distribute malware or trick people into revealing passwords or financial details.
  • Do not publish private information, account credentials, personal contact details or identifying information without permission.
  • Do not use spam, fake engagement, automated abuse or coordinated manipulation.

4. Share lawful content

  • Do not use FACEBOOM to offer illegal goods or services, or to promote illegal activity.
  • Only upload content you created or have permission and legal rights to share.
  • Do not evade restrictions by creating replacement accounts or moving prohibited activity to another feature.

5. How to report and block

If you see content or behaviour that may break these guidelines, use the Report option on the relevant post, comment or profile where it is available. Include clear details so the moderation team can review the concern.

You can also use Block / Unblock on a member's profile or content where that option is available. Blocking helps limit unwanted interactions; it does not replace reporting serious safety concerns.

For a safety concern that cannot be reported in the app, email faceboom.support@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

6. How FACEBOOM reviews reports

FACEBOOM's moderation team may review reported content, relevant account information and available context. Reviews are handled by authorized moderators. We aim to assess reports fairly and consistently, while protecting the privacy of people involved.

Submitting a report does not guarantee that content or an account will be restricted. We may take no action when a report does not show a guideline violation, or when there is not enough information to assess it.

7. Possible enforcement actions

Depending on the seriousness, context and history of the behaviour, FACEBOOM may:

  • Remove or limit access to content.
  • Issue a warning or require corrective action.
  • Temporarily restrict features or suspend an account.
  • Permanently disable an account for serious or repeated violations.
  • Preserve or escalate information where required by law or necessary to address a serious safety risk.

FACEBOOM may act without a prior warning when a violation is severe or urgent. Enforcement on FACEBOOM is decided by FACEBOOM, not automatically by Facebook.

8. Appeals

If you believe FACEBOOM made a mistake when removing your content or restricting your account, you may request a review by emailing faceboom.support@gmail.com with the subject FACEBOOM Moderation Appeal.

Include your FACEBOOM username, the date of the action, and a short explanation of why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. Do not send your password. Appeals are reviewed by an authorized person where possible. The original decision may be upheld, changed or reversed.

9. Urgent danger and child-safety concerns

FACEBOOM email is not an emergency service and is not monitored as a real-time emergency channel. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority in your location. Do not wait for an email response.

10. Contact FACEBOOM

Questions about these guidelines, safety reports and moderation appeals:

Email: faceboom.support@gmail.com

FACEBOOM may update these guidelines as the service and applicable requirements change. The version displayed on this page is the current published version.
